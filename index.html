About the artist

Meet Ritika

Ritika Mehndi Art is dedicated to creating beautiful, detailed mehndi designs for brides, families and special occasions. From traditional patterns to elegant contemporary designs, every mehndi application is created with attention to detail and the personality of the occasion.

Every booking starts with a conversation — your date, your outfit, how much coverage you want and the style you have in mind. The design is then planned around that, so it feels like yours rather than something picked off a page.
